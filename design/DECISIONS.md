# Design decisions — open questions & findings (D1–Dn)

> **The registry the `.pen` files point at.** Briefs in `flows/*.pen` and warning notes in
> `ui-kit.lib.pen` cite these by number. Keep entries after resolution — record the outcome
> instead of deleting; the reasoning is the useful part.

**Status:** read it from the board, not from here
([ADR-022](../docs/architecture/adr/0022-github-as-the-system-of-record.md)). Every unresolved
entry is a board item keyed by `Dn`:

```sh
gh project item-list 6 --owner rodacato --format json --limit 200 \
  | jq -r '.items[] | select(.title | test("^\\[D[0-9]+\\]")) | "\(.status)\t\(.title)"' | sort
```

> **The count that stood here is retired, for the reason it kept demonstrating.** It was a
> hand-kept tally of data derived from this file, and it was **wrong seven times** — four of them
> because a column was carried forward instead of re-derived. #443 retired the sibling tally in
> V2_REMAINING on 2026-08-29 and published the commands in its place; this
> follows it, and ADR-022 gives the open entries one home so there is one place to read.
>
> **What the verdict marker means now.** `✅` records that a decision was taken and names the
> outcome — history, and it stays true. `⏳` marks an entry the board owns; what state it is in
> (draft, blocked, being researched) is read there and not inferred from this column.
>
> Re-derive the totals from the file when you want them — never increment the old ones:
>
> ```sh
> awk -F'|' '/^\| \*\*D[0-9]+\*\* \|/ {print $5}' design/DECISIONS.md \
>   | grep -oE '^ *(✅|⏳)' | tr -d ' ' | sort | uniq -c
> ```
>
> **Parsing the verdict column is the part that matters.** A bare `grep '⏳'` over the whole row
> also matches a *resolved* entry that happens to cite an open `Dn`, which is how a previous
> version counted eleven.

**Citations of `CODE_CHANGES` and `V2_REMAINING`** point at two files retired on 2026-09-16
(ADR-022's amendment); read them with `git show 120600bb:design/<file>`.

**How entries work.** A `Dn` is a finding logged when a call isn't purely mechanical. During the
2.0 redesign the design led the code (D8), so many entries record forward design calls — each
with what we picked and why. **D78 retired that on 2026-09-05:** the code is the source of truth
again, and an entry the code disagrees with is amended in the artboard. An entry a later one
reverses opens with `⤷` and the number that replaced it. D111 was never registered as a row; D114
cites it as a move.

They are two things for two audiences: `TrendScore` is a continuous score **for machines** (`MarketSentiment` reads 50 of them, `AlertEvaluator` has rules on it — it cannot be removed), the semaphore is a boolean rule **for a human**. And S6 named an inconsistency that must be answered in writing rather than by omission: **X16 rejected an appended readings history while `trend_scores` is already append-only with `calculated_at` and no prune job.** **Also fixed here (C5):** "off" and "not built" currently share a visual treatment, so the user cannot tell *"this is not happening"* from *"we did not build it"*. ## Open product questions — framed, not decided

**These three are Adrian's product vision.** ON-06 is what first boot asks of a stranger; ON-10 is
whether the catalogue carries the product's stated differentiator; AU-06 is what the product's one
desktop-exclusive surface is for. A recommendation from us on any of them would be an invention
dressed as advice — there is no code fact, no ADR and no prior `Dn` that settles them, and the
evidence below narrows the options without choosing between them. **Stated plainly so it is not
mistaken for hedging: I am not recommending an option in any of the three.**

### ON-06 — what step 1 of the wizard is *for*

**The question.** Six API keys presented as equals, or one required key — Banxico, the only one the
wizard can verify — with the other five deferred to Ajustes?

**The evidence, verified.**

- `MarketData::Domain::ProviderDirectory::ENTRIES` (`provider_directory.rb:12-32`) holds **eight**
  providers: six with `requires_key: true` (DataBursatil, Alpaca, Finnhub, ExchangeRate, Banxico,
  CoinGecko) and two public (Yahoo Finance, Alternative.me).
- `onboarding/integrations.html.erb:13` partitions on `requires_key`, so the six keyed providers get
  a full card each (`:17-51`: icon tile, name, type, description, cost-of-skipping notice, labelled
  field, *dónde consigo* link) and the two public ones a compact list (`:54-72`). The wall is the six.
- `OnboardingController#load_integrations` is `Integration.order(:provider_name)`
  (`onboarding_controller.rb:55-57`) — **alphabetical**. Banxico is second because B follows A, at
  the same weight as the other five.
- **Only Banxico is exercised.** `SaveApiKeys:20-24` sets `banxico ||= …` and returns
  `fx: banxico ? pull_fx_history : :skipped`; `pull_fx_history:31-35` calls
  `MarketData::UseCases::SyncFxHistory` and reports `:failed` on failure, which
  `render_fx_failure` now renders on step 1 itself (ON-03, since fixed).
- **Every other key is recorded as verified without being tested.** `save_api_keys.rb:18-19` writes
  the value and then sets `connection_status: :connected` for any non-blank string, so `hola` typed
  into Finnhub surfaces green in Ajustes forever after.
- A per-provider verify already exists on the other surface:
  `admin/integrations/_provider_card.html.erb` carries *Verificar conexión*, and
  `refresh_sync_admin_integration_path` is routed.
- The skip control (`omitir`, `:75`) sits at the **bottom** of the wall, after all six cards.
- Banxico is the one key the multi-currency thesis rests on: without it,
  *"un movimiento con fecha pasada se valúa al tipo de cambio de hoy, no al del día en que
  ocurrió"* (`provider_directory.rb:23`).

**The real options.**

1. **Six equals, ordered by consequence rather than alphabet.** Keep the wall, replace
   `order(:provider_name)` with an explicit order that puts Banxico first. Cheapest change; the wall
   and the unverified-green problem both stay.
2. **One required, five deferred.** Step 1 asks for Banxico alone, verifies it on the spot (the code
   already does), and hands the rest to Ajustes › Integraciones, which is built, has the cards, and
   has the verify button. Step 1 stops being a wall; the reader reaches Panorama sooner with a
   correct FX foundation and no market data.
3. **One required, five offered but collapsed.** Banxico expanded and verified; the other five
   behind a single disclosure, still fillable in place for someone who arrived with their keys ready.
4. **Keep six, add verification to all six.** Answers the honesty half (ON-02) and not the wall half;
   turns step 1 into six network calls at first boot.

**What each trades.** Option 2 is the only one that makes step 1 short, and it is the only one that
guarantees a fresh instance opens with no prices at all until the reader goes back to Ajustes —
which is the hand-off that success criterion #2 measures. Option 1 is a two-line change that leaves
the ~5,292px wall exactly as tall. Ordering is downstream of whichever is chosen, not a separate
question. **The layout half of ON-06 is not here** — it folded into the audit's thesis B `D<n>`,
which asks whether the product has a desktop layer at all.

for, which is Adrian's to say. **DS-08 pairs with it.**

### AU-06 — what the product's one desktop-exclusive surface is for

**The question.** `layouts/auth.html.erb:13` is the only place in Stockerly that renders content
that exists *only* on desktop, rather than reflowing mobile content. What should it hold?

**The evidence, verified.**

- `layouts/auth.html.erb:13-23` is `<aside class="hidden flex-col justify-between bg-primary p-8 lg:flex">`
  with exactly **two** children: the wordmark link (`:16-18`) and one check-glyph line reading
  `t("auth.open_source")` (`:19-22`). It fills one half of a `max-w-4xl lg:grid-cols-2` card
  (`:10`). Between the two children is flat `bg-primary`.
- It is genuinely the only one. The audit's thesis B surveyed `app/views/<dir>` for `lg:` and found
  the entire vocabulary is reflow — `grid-cols-12`, `col-span-*`, `order-*`, `flex-row`, `w-80` —
  with `signals`, `onboarding`, `welcome`, `setup`, `alerts` and `settings` carrying **none**.
  `layouts/` was outside that survey, which is why this is the single exception rather than a
  contradiction of the count.
- **Transplant test:** an indigo half-panel holding a logo and a one-line tagline pastes into any
  SaaS login unchanged.
- **Removal test:** delete the `<aside>` and the card becomes one column and the form centres.
  Nothing is lost but brand presence — which is real, and is the whole argument for keeping it.
- Every screen under this layout is an auth screen: `sessions/new`, `setup/new`,
  `password_resets/{new,edit,sent,expired,success}`, `two_factor/*`.
- `setup/new.html.erb:39` prints **the same** `auth.open_source` key the panel prints, so on
  `/setup` at `lg` the tagline renders twice, two thirds of a card apart (**AU-03**).
- **BR-01's contrast failure is fixed and the panel is still empty.** The brand pass measured the
  symbol at 1.00:1 in dark; `:17` now renders `brand_logo(:dark)` with `brightness-0 invert`, and
  the comment at `:14-15` records the reasoning. The half that carries the mark is now visible —
  which means AU-06 is now *exactly* as empty as it measures, and no emptier.

**The real options — and the audit named a third that is genuinely defensible, which is why this is
a decision rather than a fix.**

1. **Give it content only Stockerly can write.** The one screen a stranger meets before the product
   exists, on the only surface where there is room: what the instance is, that it is theirs, that
   nothing leaves it. Fails the transplant test on purpose. Costs copy nobody has written, and it
   must not become a marketing panel on a self-hosted box with an audience of one.
2. **Shrink the card to the form.** Drop the `<aside>`, drop `lg:grid-cols-2`, centre a single
   column. Deletes the emptiness and the one desktop-exclusive surface with it. Also retires AU-03's
   duplicate for free.
3. **Keep the void deliberately, and record it as a choice.** Brand quiet on the one screen in the
   product that is correctly bare. `justify-between` with two children is then the intended
   composition, not the `AC-02` defect family — and the difference between those two readings is a
   sentence in this registry, not a diff.

**What nobody should decide by default.** Today it is option 3 *by accident* — nothing records that
the emptiness is intentional, so every future craft pass will file it again. Whichever of the three
is chosen, the choice is what closes the finding; the pixels are downstream.
