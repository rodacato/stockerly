> **The registry the `.pen` files point at.** Briefs in `flows/*.pen` and warning notes in
> `ui-kit.lib.pen` cite these by number. Keep entries after resolution — record the outcome
> instead of deleting; the reasoning is the useful part.

**Status:** 27 entries · 21 resolved · 6 open · 3 🔴 high-impact · 8 🐞 app bugs (4 fixed)

**How entries work.** A `Dn` is a finding logged when a call isn't purely mechanical. In a
normal migration the design matches the code; here (a **redesign**) the design leads on visual
values, so entries also record forward design calls — each with what we picked and why.

| **D27** | Backdated trades and a snapshot history that is never rebuilt | Backfill snapshots · Derive history from trades · Accept drift | 🔴 **Open — the real blocker under slice 4, measured 2026-08-24.** `TakeSnapshotsJob` writes **only today's** snapshot and is idempotent per day; nothing anywhere backfills or recomputes an earlier one (`grep snapshot app/jobs lib/tasks` finds no other writer). Meanwhile the trade sheet ships a `date_field :executed_at` and the contract validates nothing — `optional(:executed_at).maybe(:string)`. **Reproduced:** record a buy dated 5 days ago, and the snapshot for that date still reads `total_value=0.0, invested=0.0` while the portfolio today holds 1,000. The snapshot history is therefore internally inconsistent with the trades, and TWR compares `V_t` against `V_{t-1} + flow` — it needs both sides to describe the same portfolio. This already corrupts `/portfolio`'s performance chart silently. It also collides head-on with the 2.0's own data-entry plan (`docs/vision/`: smart CSV, skip-history, holdings snapshot) — **importing history is bulk-inserting backdated trades**. 🐞 **Second, smaller:** a trade dated 90 days in the **future** is accepted without complaint (reproduced). Fixing D12 without this only changes which wrong number is displayed. |
